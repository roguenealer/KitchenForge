KitchenForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Overview

KitchenForge ("the App") is a food pantry management and recipe discovery application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is used.

Data Collection

Pantry items, preferences, spending data, and photo-recognition results are stored locally on your device. KitchenForge does not upload your food or receipt photos. The Android scanner uses Google ML Kit, which collects the diagnostic information described below.

Permissions Used

  • Camera and selected photos: Used only when you take a photo or choose an image to read food labels or receipts. Recognition runs on-device. On Android, a captured photo is kept in a temporary private cache file while it is processed and deleted when the scan finishes or is cancelled. The app does not upload photos or save captured photos to your gallery. Choosing a library image grants access to that selected image, not your entire library.
  • Microphone: Used only for voice-to-text input when you choose to add items by speaking. Audio is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted by the App.
  • Internet: Used for online recipes, web fonts, and Android scanner diagnostics. Food and receipt photos are not sent to a recognition server.

Third-Party Services

The App uses TheMealDB API (themealdb.com) for recipe searches and images. Search terms and ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, are visible to that provider. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Android photo recognition: Bundled Google ML Kit text recognition and image labeling process photos locally. The SDK collects device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, configuration and image dimensions, input/output sizes, feature events, and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. This diagnostic data is encrypted in transit. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure. The iOS scanner uses Apple's on-device Vision framework.

Data Storage

Pantry items, spending history, preferences, and unfinished photo-review drafts are kept in local app storage. KitchenForge does not provide a cloud account or pantry-sync service. Device backups may include app data according to your operating system and backup settings. The Android app retains a local recovery copy when upgrading older pantry storage.

Children's Privacy

KitchenForge does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App does not require account creation or personal information of any kind.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Google Play Store listing.
